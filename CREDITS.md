# Credits

## OSCAT - Open Source Community for Automation Technology

This project, `beebread`, is a direct port of the excellent and widely-used OSCAT libraries from their original IEC 61131-3 Structured Text to the Go programming language. The goal of `beebread` is to make the robust and time-tested logic of OSCAT available to the modern Go ecosystem.

All credit for the original algorithms, design, and years of refinement belongs to the OSCAT community and its dedicated contributors.
